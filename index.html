🔍 What's the Problem?

Every day at school pickup, things get messy on Main Street! 😬

  • 🚸 The shop side has no sidewalk — kids walk right in the road.
  • 🚗 Cars and scooters stop illegally, blocking the way.
  • 🌳 No trees or lights make it hot, dark and unsafe.
  • ♿ Wheelchairs and strollers have nowhere safe to go.
  • 🧩 You can only use 20 pieces in total — choose smartly!

💡 Design Tips

  • Sidewalks on both sides keep people safe.
  • Pick-up zones stop illegal parking.
  • Trees make the street cooler and greener.
  • Solar lights save energy and keep it bright.
  • Share space fairly between people, bikes and cars.
